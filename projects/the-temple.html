The Temple

3D modelling and animation
Rok Pat
Artwork featured in the environment
SUDANIM

Rok designed and modelled this Japan-inspired temple for a promotional film featuring tattoo artist SUDANIM. The rooms incorporate the artist’s framed work into the architecture and set dressing.

Most of the environment was modelled and textured in Blender and Substance Painter, with additional assets from Quixel Megascans.
