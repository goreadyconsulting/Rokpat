Yi Jing began when pandemic restrictions made live gigs impossible. Rok, Fifi Rong and their collaborators built an online series that combined the atmosphere of a concert with short film and video art.

Each episode used a different visual world to present familiar songs and premieres. The team developed the first season over six months, with the finale in June 2021.
